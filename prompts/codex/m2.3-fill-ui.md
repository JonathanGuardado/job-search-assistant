## Task: Review an application, open it filled in the browser, and follow the fill to the end

**Milestone:** 2.3
**Read first:** `AGENTS.md`, `docs/conventions.md`, `docs/decisions/0016-form-filling.md` (all of it), `docs/domain-model.md` (section `application_fills`), and `prompts/codex/m2.2-drafts-ui.md` (the job page this task extends)

### Goal

The system can now fill a Greenhouse application form in a visible browser window, from a fixed packet the owner reviewed, and record what was sent (decision 0016). This task builds the pages the owner uses for that:
1. On a "Can apply" job, the owner presses "Review and fill".
2. They see exactly what the browser will enter, and what is left for them.
3. They press "Open the filled form", and a browser window opens with the form filled.
4. They finish the form there and press Submit in that window.
5. The page then follows the fill to its end. If the system cannot tell whether the application was submitted, the owner settles it with "I submitted it" or "I didn't".

- **Do not write to the dev database, and do not start fills yourself.** Starting one opens a real browser on a real employer's form. Check your work with the commands under "Definition of done". The owner runs the manual checks.

### Implementation notes

**Today's date** for `appliedOn` everywhere: `new Date().toISOString().slice(0, 10)`.

**Job page, section "Fill the application".**
- **Where:** directly above "Your application".
- **When to show it:** only when `tab === 'can_apply'` and `application === null`.
- **What it reads:** `latestFillForJob(scope, jobId)`, inside the existing `withTenant` call.
- **What it shows:**
  - **No fill, or one that is `abandoned`, `failed` or `stale`:** a form posting to `prepareFillAction`, with hidden `jobId` and the button "Review and fill". Under it, the line "The assistant fills the form in a browser window on this computer. You check it and press Submit yourself."
  - **Otherwise:** one line by status, plus the link "Open" to `/jobs/<jobId>/apply/<fillId>`:
    - `prepared`: "Reviewed, not started."
    - `queued`, `launching`, `filling`: "Opening the form…"
    - `waiting`: "Filled: finish it in the browser window."
    - `uncertain`: "Was it submitted? Tell the assistant."
    - `submitted`: "Submitted."

**Review and status page** `/jobs/[jobId]/apply/[fillId]`.
- **Inputs:** validate both params with `z.uuid()`; otherwise `notFound()`.
- **On every load, before reading,** inside `withTenant`:
  1. `expireFills(scope)`
  2. `finishPending(scope, today)`
  3. `getFill(scope, fillId)`; when it returns `null`, or a fill whose `jobId` is not this page's, `notFound()`.
- **Header:** the job title and company (from `getJob`, as on the job page), and a link back to the job.
- **`error` and `notice` search parameters:** shown as on the job page.
- **Refresh:** while `status` is `queued`, `launching`, `filling` or `waiting`, render `<meta httpEquiv="refresh" content="5" />` so the page follows the worker. No client components.

**By status:**

1. **`prepared`.** Under the heading "What the browser will enter", one list per kind of packet item, in packet order:
   - **"Filled"** (`kind: 'fill'`), one row per item:
     - the label, then the value with `<AnswerValue value={item.value} />`;
     - the source: details "From your details", resume "Your resume", profile "From your profile", saved "Your saved answer";
     - for saved answers, also a badge by origin: owner "Written by you", profile_draft "From a profile draft you accepted", written_draft "From a written draft you accepted".
   - **"Left for you"** (`kind: 'unresolved'`): the label and the reason:
     - unanswered: "Required, and not answered yet."
     - option_missing: "Your saved answer is not one of this form's options."
     - own_words: "This employer asks for your own words; your saved answer came from a draft."
     - consent: "A consent you give yourself on the form."

     Under this list: "You'll finish these in the browser window before you press Submit." Hide the list when it is empty.
   - **"Left empty"** (`kind: 'empty'`): labels only, under the heading "Optional, left empty". Hide it when it is empty.
   - **Expiry:** "This review can be started until <createdAt + PREPARED_TTL_MS, formatted with `toLocaleString('en-US')`>."
   - **Buttons:**
     - A form posting to `startFillAction`, with hidden `fillId`, `jobId` and `packetHash` (= `fill.packetHash`) and the button "Open the filled form". Under it: "A browser window opens on this computer, with the form filled. Nothing is sent until you press Submit in that window."
     - A form posting to `cancelFillAction` (hidden `fillId`, `jobId`), button "Cancel".
2. **`queued`, `launching`, `filling`.** "Opening the form in a browser window…" and the Cancel form. Cancel fails once the browser is open; show that error as returned.
3. **`waiting`.**
   - The line "The form is filled. Check it in the browser window, finish what is listed below, and press Submit there."
   - The fill report, under "What the assistant could not do": every `FieldResult` whose `result` is not `set`. Each row shows the label, or the field when the label is empty, and the `detail`. Mark `required` ones with a "Required" badge. Then the count line "<n> fields filled".
   - A form posting to `settleFillAction` with hidden `fillId`, `jobId` and `said=submitted`, button "I submitted it". Under it: "Use this if you submitted and this page has not noticed."
4. **`uncertain`.**
   - The line "The assistant cannot tell whether this application was submitted."
   - `fill.error` when set.
   - When `fill.submission` is set: "Your browser sent the application at <capturedAt, local time>" and "Greenhouse's answer: <response status, or 'none seen'>".
   - Two forms posting to `settleFillAction`: `said=submitted`, button "I submitted it"; and `said=not_submitted`, button "I didn't submit it".
   - The note "Until you say, the assistant will not open this job's form again."
5. **`submitted`.**
   - "Submitted", and by `outcome`: confirmed "Greenhouse confirmed it."; user_reported "You said you submitted it."
   - When `outcomeEvidence.kind === 'confirmation_page'`, show its `text` in a `<blockquote>`.
   - When `applicationId` is set, the link "Open the application" to `/applications/<applicationId>`. When it is not, the line "Recording the application… refresh in a moment."
   - The report of what was not set, as for `waiting`, under "What the assistant could not do".
6. **`failed`, `abandoned`, `stale`.**
   - One line by status:
     - failed: "This fill failed."
     - abandoned: "This fill was stopped before anything was sent."
     - stale: "What you reviewed changed before the browser opened."
   - `fill.error` when set.
   - When `outcome === 'rejected'` and `outcomeEvidence.kind === 'error'`, also "Greenhouse refused it" and the evidence `text`.
   - A form posting to `prepareFillAction` (hidden `jobId`), button "Review again".

**Server actions** (`src/app/jobs/[jobId]/apply/actions.ts`, `'use server'`). Each parses its inputs with Zod: `jobId` and `fillId` with `z.uuid()`, `packetHash` with `z.string().min(1)`, `said` with `z.enum(['submitted', 'not_submitted'])`.
- **Where they redirect:**
  - An action that fails before a fill exists redirects to `/jobs/<jobId>?error=<message>`.
  - Every other one redirects to `/jobs/<jobId>/apply/<fillId>`, plus `?error=` or `?notice=` as below.
- **No `redirect` inside a `try`.** Messages come from `result.error.message`.

The actions:
- **`prepareFillAction`:** `prepareFill(getDb(), DEV_USER.id, jobId)`. On success, go to the fill's page. On error, go to the job page with the error.
- **`startFillAction`:**
  1. `startFill(scope, fillId, packetHash)`; on error, back to the fill page with the error.
  2. Then `queueFill(getEnv().REDIS_URL, DEV_USER.id, fillId)`. If that fails, call `cancelFill(scope, fillId)` and redirect with "The worker could not be reached. Is `pnpm worker` running? Nothing was opened or sent."
  3. On success, redirect with notice "Opening the form in a browser window on this computer."
- **`cancelFillAction`:** `cancelFill`, then redirect with its error, or notice "Stopped. Nothing was sent."
- **`settleFillAction`:** two separate `withTenant` calls.
  1. `settleFill(scope, fillId, said)`; on error, redirect with it.
  2. When `said === 'submitted'`, then `finishRecording(scope, fillId, today)` in a second `withTenant`. A failure there redirects with notice "Recorded that you submitted it. The application will be recorded when you next open this page." plus the error message.
  3. Otherwise, redirect with notice "Recorded." Never combine the two writes in one `withTenant`.

Forms work without client JavaScript. Follow the existing CSS module styles.
